LIFE COCKPIT / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

How Life Cockpit accesses, uses, stores and transfers data, and how its owner can stop access or request deletion.

Effective and updated: September 27, 2026 · Applies to the personal-use instance maintained by SnapPython

1. Scope and operator

Life Cockpit is a calendar, task and information assistant maintained by SnapPython for personal use. It has no public registration. This public website provides information and policies only; it does not connect to your Google account.

Contact: eringhong30@gmail.com

2. Data accessed and purposes

  • Google Calendar: authorized access to calendar identifiers, event titles, descriptions, start/end times and other event information for agenda views, planning and task-related event synchronization. Related events may be created, updated or deleted through the owner's actions and enabled synchronization rules.
  • Google Tasks: the tasks scope is requested. The application normally synchronizes the designated Life Cockpit list, including titles, notes, due dates and completion status. Tasks may be created, updated, completed or deleted through owner actions and confirmed plans.
  • Gmail: a separate gmail.readonly grant imports recent message identifiers, senders, subjects, received times and bodies for organization, summaries and task suggestions. The first sync covers the previous seven days; later syncs use an incremental checkpoint. This is not limited to the Inbox label and excludes Spam, Trash, Sent and Drafts. Attachments are not downloaded. This integration does not send, delete, mark read or archive email. Formal tasks follow the owner confirmation workflow.
  • Account and connection information: connected account identifiers, granted permissions, OAuth tokens and synchronization status to access authorized services, refresh access and display connection health.
  • Owner input and derived content: submitted tasks, notes, files or messages, along with summaries, task suggestions, confirmation records, briefings and delivery states that support the personal workspace.

Calendar requests calendar.events. The Google consent screen shows the actual permissions requested. Calendar/Tasks and Gmail use separate connection flows.

3. AI, Feishu and service providers

AI inputs may also include project progress, reminders, training summaries and inbox context. The server configuration selects the model provider; implemented routes support xAI and OpenAI. This page does not indicate that every provider is enabled.

When the owner enables relevant features, selected email text, calendar or task content, submitted messages and derived summaries may pass through the server's AI gateway to configured model providers for summarization, task extraction, clarification questions and briefing generation. This is model inference; it does not run entirely in the browser or on the owner's local device.

When Feishu or email notifications are enabled, task summaries, questions, reminders and briefings are sent to the owner's configured accounts or channels. Google receives requests needed to perform calendar and task operations. Hosting, backup and configured processing services may also store data needed to operate these features. Enabled Feishu synchronization may also transfer task titles, dates, execution times and status, and ordinary event titles, descriptions, locations, times and source links. Messages captured through Feishu may be stored in the application inbox.

Provider processing locations, logs and retention depend on the services and settings selected. This policy does not promise zero retention by third parties. Before enabling a provider, the owner should review its data-use terms and select only services consistent with the limited-use commitments below.

4. Limited use of Google data

Google user data and derived content are used only for the personal calendar, task, message organization and reminder features disclosed here. Life Cockpit does not sell this data or use it for advertising profiles, credit scoring or training general-purpose AI models.

Transfers to processors must be limited to providing features the owner has enabled and consented to, or necessary security and legal purposes. Private content is not made available for human review except by the owner or for necessary processing with their explicit agreement.

Life Cockpit's use of information received from Google APIs, and its transfer to other applications, must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

The implemented backup policy generates daily full-history business-record JSON snapshots. Local daily snapshots are retained for about 30 days; monthly snapshots and the configured private Git archive have no automatic expiry rule. Backups exclude OAuth credentials but may include imported email bodies and derived content. Backup file contents are not application-encrypted. Whether backups run depends on instance configuration.

The application server stores synchronized snapshots, business records, generated content, queues and synchronization metadata. OAuth credentials are encrypted on the server; this does not mean every business record or backup is individually encrypted. Application access requires authentication, and Google API requests use HTTPS.

Business records remain for the operation of enabled features until an applicable deletion action is taken or the maintainer processes deletion. The current implementation has no single automatic deletion period covering all data types. Backups and third-party copies require separate handling; immediate deletion is not promised.

6. Revocation and deletion

You can disconnect Calendar/Tasks or Gmail in the application, or remove access through Google Account third-party connections. Check each connection if they were authorized separately.

Disconnection and deletion are different operations. Stopping further access does not automatically remove all synchronized snapshots, generated briefings, delivery records or backups. It does not automatically delete original content in Google or Feishu.

To request deletion of local data, contact the maintainer at the email above and identify the connection or data categories concerned. Application records, derived content and manageable backups must be handled separately; provider copies are also subject to the relevant service's procedures. Do not submit private email, tokens or account records in public GitHub issues.

7. This public website and updates

This information site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not load analytics, advertising scripts or Google sign-in components and does not read Life Cockpit business data. GitHub may process requests, IP addresses and technical logs under its privacy statement.

The date and content of this policy will be updated when features or data uses change. New data access or uses require notice and consent where applicable.
