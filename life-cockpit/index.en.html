LIFE COCKPIT / PERSONAL WORKSPACE

A clearer day,
from plans to follow-ups.

Life Cockpit is a personal-use assistant maintained by SnapPython. It helps its owner organize their Google Calendar, tasks and email, review daily plans, and generate task suggestions and daily briefings.

Privacy and data use
01 / CALENDAR

See the day ahead

After authorization, read Google Calendar events alongside local time blocks and task schedules. Create or update related calendar events through enabled task synchronization rules.

02 / TASKS

Keep tasks in sync

Synchronize Google Tasks and completion states, turn confirmed plans into tasks, and track follow-ups.

03 / MESSAGES

Find what needs attention

With separate Gmail authorization, extract task candidates from messages matching the configured filters. Enabled AI and Feishu features provide summaries, clarification questions and reminders.

A private workspace for one person

The application is for its maintainer's personal use and has no public registration. This website contains only product information and a privacy policy. It does not display calendars, email or tasks, and does not request Google authorization.

The owner starts a connection in their own application instance and completes sign-in and consent on Google's pages. See the privacy policy for AI processing, Feishu delivery and data retention.
